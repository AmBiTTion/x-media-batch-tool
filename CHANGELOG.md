# Changelog / 更新日志

## v1.2.0 — 2026-10-20 (GitHub release / GitHub 首次发布)

### English

- Download controls and save-type menu under each post, including a batch-selection checkbox.
- Collapsible panel with post, task, and settings tabs; light/dark and narrow-screen layouts.
- Sequential queue, progress, automatic retry, manual recovery, duplicate skipping, and forced re-download.
- JSON/CSV exports, optional TXT sidecars, filename presets, and persistent settings/history.
- Reproducible extension build, unit/browser tests, bilingual documentation, and unpacked-extension/userscript release assets.
- Known limits: actual Android compatibility and logged-in real-media downloads are unverified; full-account/profile archives and dedicated repost-type filters are not included.

### 简体中文

- 每帖下载按钮、保存类型菜单及加入批量复选框。
- 默认收起的帖子/任务/设置面板、深浅主题及窄屏布局。
- 顺序队列、进度、自动重试、手动恢复、去重和强制重下。
- JSON/CSV 导出、可选 TXT 正文、命名预设、设置与历史保存。
- 可复现构建、单元与浏览器测试、中英文说明、扩展与油猴发布文件。
- 已知限制：未验证安卓实机及登录账号真实媒体下载；不包含整账号/账号资料归档或转发类型专用筛选。
